Phase 3 progress: `src/mastery.ts` (pure, tested in `scripts/test-mastery.ts`, run by `npm test`) computes a level per line from the attempt log plus the line's FSRS stability. Per position: a clean recall = one correct attempt, no hint, at most 10 s, with no wrong move earlier in the same visit; the position score counts distinct days with a clean recall; a slip takes one off (never below zero). Line level uses the weakest position: Learning (attempted), Familiar (every position clean on at least 1 day), Solid (2 days and stability of 7+ days), Mastered (3 days, stability of 21+ days, and a clean out-of-order recall at half or more of the positions). All thresholds are placeholders in `src/mastery.ts`. FSRS is still per line (one card per variation), so stability is line-level while day counts are per position. Levels show on the opening page (tag plus a one-line next step) and on the home cards ("n solid"). Drill now adds a spot check after a familiar line, once a day: the line starts from a random position in the middle and only logs attempts (no FSRS review, so it never reschedules the line). Deferred on purpose: the "why" question gate, because there is no why-question content or UI yet, so Mastered currently needs the numbers plus out-of-order recall only. Verified in a real browser (seeded progress, played a full drill line by clicking the board, saw the spot check start mid-line).

Phase 4a progress (rule-based mistakes tracker): the attempt log now records the wrong move played (`Attempt.s`, wrong attempts only). `src/mistakes.ts` explains an attempt from verified facts only: the move belongs to another line at the same point (`other-line`), belongs later in this line (`move-order`), is a capture or check (`tactical`, reusing `whyNot` from `src/why.ts` and the engine `caps`), or is just another move (`other`); a hint or a slow correct move gets its own text. It never names the line's own move. `troubleSpots` ranks positions from the last 30 days (wrong move = 1, hint = 1, slow = 0.5) and drops a position once its last two attempts are clean. ## Mastery definition (decided; thresholds are placeholders to tune with real data)
- Mastery is per position; a line is capped by its weakest few positions, not the average.
- Position strength comes from FSRS stability in days: under 3, 3-14, 14-45, over 45 map to the upper levels.
- A correct recall counts only if unaided (no hint) and at a normal speed.
- Each level step needs correct recalls on separate days (3 for Mastered).
- Solid and Mastered also need out-of-order starts and a short "why" question.
- Soft slip (owner decision): a mistake lowers that position's strength through FSRS and flags it. It does not reset the whole line or its day count.
- No decay penalty while away; the level shows what was proven, and reviews come due as normal.
- Research notes: FSRS defines no "mastered" threshold (each app picks one). Chessbook scores mastery from review intervals (about 100 days = full credit). Successive relearning (correct to criterion across spaced sessions) is the supported idea; evidence on interleaving is mixed.

## Lessons
- Keep the message card at a fixed height. A taller message shifts the board and Chessground's cached bounds send taps to the wrong squares.
- Don't run `pkill -f <pattern>` with a pattern that also appears in your own command line; it kills the shell.
- Don't name the line's own move in "why not" text; it would give the answer away in Drill.
